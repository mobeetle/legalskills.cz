Privacy policy

How legalskills.cz handles personal data.

1. What we process

Enquiries: details you give us (name, email, organisation, message). Marketing: email + consent record, only if you opt in. Technical data: analytics and marketing storage only with your consent (see Cookie policy).

2. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR)

Enquiries: pre-contractual steps (Art. 6(1)(b)). Newsletters: only with consent (Art. 6(1)(a), § 7 of Act No. 480/2004 Coll.). Analytics: only with consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). Site security: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

3. Marketing consent

Wording: “I agree that Metropolitan advisory s.r.o. may send me commercial communications about legalskills.cz news (approx. 1× a month) to my email for 3 years. Consent is voluntary and I may withdraw it anytime at hello@legalskills.cz.” Withdrawal is as easy as giving consent. We keep the consent record for 3 years as proof.

4. Retention

Enquiries: handling + 3 years (limitation period). Marketing data: until withdrawal, max 3 years. Then deleted unless the law requires longer.

5. Recipients

We never sell data. We share it only with our hosting provider and tools you consented to (analytics, mailing). No automated decision-making or profiling with legal effects.

6. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent. Write to hello@legalskills.cz — we reply within a month. You may complain to the Czech DPA (uoou.cz).

7. Local run

The LegalSkills suite runs locally inside your AI agent and sends us nothing by itself. Your AI agent's terms apply too.

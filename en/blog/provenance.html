Provenance tags: the audit trail

Five tags separate what is in the document, what is authority, what is inferred and what you said.

Legal work fails when what is in the document, what the law says and what the model guessed get mixed together. The suite forbids that: every claim and every map node carries a provenance tag.

  • DOC verbatim from the source document — checked by substance-preservation.
  • AUTH external authority — must resolve, or it is a grounding failure.
  • INFER derived reasoning — name the premises.
  • USER a fact supplied by the user.
  • ASSUMED an unverified default — surfaced at a checkpoint.

Why it is not cosmetic

The tags drive the checks. An unresolved authority is downgraded to ASSUMED and surfaced — never silently passed. A claim with no provenance is visible at a glance, even in someone else's work.

Never invent provisions, citations or case law. An unsourced claim is a claim, not a fact.

What the set does

Legal skills for your agent

The core is free — from first read to calm signature. Tailored playbooks, workflows and skills on request.

/legal

Describe in your own words what you need — even a messy brief. It tidies the goals, proposes a Gate Plan to approve and asks when something is missing.

Start here

/legal-intake

Checks if the matter fits — scope, conflicts, economics, capacity, urgency. Turns the story into facts, parties and deadlines and opens the file.

New inquiry without chaos

/legal-research

Produces legal research grounded in statutes and case law — all with a citation trail.

Answers with sources

/legal-review

Drafts and reviews documents. Tracks working versions across negotiation rounds, including comments and redlines. Describes risks, proposes fixes and explains them plainly.

Contracts, drafts and checks

/legal-dispute

Analyses the file, prepares timeline and arguments. Watches deadlines and preclusion, flags missing evidence, exposes weak spots. Anticipates the other side's case and how to answer it.

When a dispute lands

/legal-project

Breaks a big matter into tasks, tracks completion and live status. Hands the file over and closes it.

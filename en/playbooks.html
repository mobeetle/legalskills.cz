Set once, reuse forever

A playbook is your negotiation standard in writing: for each clause the wording you table first, the compromise you accept and the line you never cross. Define it once — the assistant applies it on every deal, even for juniors.

Three tiers per position
Preferred
The ideal wording you table first.
Fallback
The compromise when they push back.
Walk-away
Beyond this line you do not sign.

Where you concede

You see the ideal, the compromise and the line you never cross.

One language

Same terms in every contract.

Red lines

What you never sign — watched for you.

Less haggling

The team knows what to hold. Deals close faster.

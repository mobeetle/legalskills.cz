How it works

Skills for your agent

No new tool. Add the skills to the agent you already use.

1

1. Add the skills

The skill set drops into any agent supporting skills — e.g. Claude, Codex or OpenCode. Every run starts with a Gate Plan to approve.

2

2. Hand over a document

Contract, filing, policy — or a whole matter. It sweeps your house context and reuse vocabulary first, then works in .legal/.

3

3. Send calmly

Risks, fixes and the other-side view on one page. Finished work ships as .docx too — with FILES and a Progress box.

4

Your files stay with you

Your files stay with you. Research queries leave anonymised.
